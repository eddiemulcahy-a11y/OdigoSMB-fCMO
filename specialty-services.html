Specialty Services

Project work, on tap.

Eight specialty areas, project-based pricing, layered onto any program. The campaigns, productions, and builds that don't belong on a monthly retainer.

Project-based · Add as needed · Specialist-delivered

The model

Project-based work, scoped one engagement at a time.

Specialty Services are the work that doesn't fit neatly into a monthly retainer — campaigns with a defined start and end, productions that ship and are done, integrations that solve a specific problem. They layer onto any program, priced per engagement, delivered by specialists. You pay for what you scope.

Eight specialty areas.

Each category covers a distinct kind of work. Pricing ranges reflect typical scope — exact pricing is set during scoping based on what your specific engagement requires.

Specialty 01

Advertising.

$1,500–$4,500/mo

Plus ad spend, billed separately

When to use it

When organic visibility isn't moving fast enough, when you need to test a new market or product, or when seasonality demands paid presence to capture demand. Best layered onto Get Found or Win Customers — paid amplifies, it doesn't replace.

What's typically delivered

  • Google Ads (Search, Performance Max)
  • Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram)
  • Yelp Ads (service businesses)
  • Local Service Ads (LSAs)
  • Audience research and targeting
  • Creative production
  • Landing page builds
  • Weekly optimization
  • Monthly performance reporting

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Local services LSA program with $3,500/mo retainer plus $4K/mo ad spend; e-commerce Meta Ads launch at $2,000/mo retainer plus $8K/mo spend; multi-location Google Ads management at $4,500/mo retainer plus $15K/mo spend.

Specialty 02

Video Production.

$2,500–$15,000/project

Scope-dependent

When to use it

When your story needs motion to land — testimonials, brand films, product demos, training content, social-native short-form. Video earns its cost when it gets used across multiple channels: site, social, ads, sales, internal. One-off videos rarely pay back.

What's typically delivered

  • On-site shoot (half-day or full-day)
  • Director and DP
  • Lighting and audio capture
  • Drone footage where relevant
  • Editorial and post-production
  • Color grading and audio mix
  • Multi-format cutdowns (16:9, 9:16, 1:1)
  • Captions and accessibility versions
  • Source files and licensed music

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Single-day testimonial shoot producing 3–5 finished pieces at $4,500; brand film project at $9,500; quarterly social video subscription at $6,000/quarter for 6–8 short-form pieces.

Specialty 03

Animation.

$1,500–$12,000/project

Style and length dependent

When to use it

When you need to explain something — a process, a product, a concept that doesn't film well — or when you want a brand expression that isn't tied to physical reality. Motion graphics for social, explainer videos for sales, brand motion for hero moments on the site.

What's typically delivered

  • Concept and storyboard
  • Script and narration
  • Custom illustration or 2D animation
  • Motion graphics and kinetic type
  • Logo and brand motion systems
  • Character animation (where in scope)
  • Voiceover production and sync
  • Sound design and music
  • Final delivery in multiple formats

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: 30-second product explainer at $4,200; brand motion logo system at $2,800; 90-second customer-onboarding animation at $9,500.

Specialty 04

Photography.

$1,200–$5,000/shoot

Per shoot day plus licensing

When to use it

When stock photos are undermining your brand, when you've launched something new, or when your team and space are visual assets you're not using. Every site, social account, and ad benefits from real photography. Most small businesses underinvest here and pay for it in conversion.

What's typically delivered

  • Pre-shoot creative direction
  • Shot list and storyboarding
  • On-location photography
  • Product and detail photography
  • Headshots and team imagery
  • Lifestyle and environmental shots
  • Editing and color treatment
  • Image library delivery
  • Web, print, and social-ready exports

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Half-day brand shoot at $1,800 producing 40+ edited images; full-day product and lifestyle shoot at $3,500; quarterly brand library refresh at $4,500/quarter across two shoot days.

Specialty 05

Earned Media.

$2,000–$6,000/mo retainer

Ongoing engagement, 6-month minimum

When to use it

When you have a story worth telling and you want it told somewhere other than your own channels — local press, industry publications, podcasts, influencer partnerships. Earned media takes longer than paid and costs more than organic, but the credibility transfer is hard to replicate any other way.

What's typically delivered

  • Story development and angle work
  • Press list building and management
  • Media pitching and outreach
  • Press release writing and distribution
  • Podcast booking and prep
  • Influencer identification and outreach
  • Partnership negotiation
  • Coverage tracking and reporting
  • Reactive media response (news cycles)

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Local PR retainer at $2,500/mo for service business; product launch PR push at $5,500/mo over 3 months; influencer partnership program at $4,000/mo with separate creator fees.

Specialty 06

Website Builds.

$5,000–$35,000/project

Scope-dependent; phased payment

When to use it

When your current site is actively losing you customers — slow, ugly, hard to update, broken on mobile, missing the conversion paths your business actually needs. A new site is a 2–3 month project that affects every other piece of marketing for years afterward. Worth getting right.

What's typically delivered

  • Discovery and information architecture
  • Sitemap and wireframes
  • Visual design and design system
  • Front-end development
  • CMS implementation (Webflow, WordPress, custom)
  • E-commerce setup (Shopify, BigCommerce)
  • Booking and form integrations
  • SEO migration and 301 mapping
  • Performance and accessibility QA
  • Training and handoff documentation

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Service-business marketing site rebuild at $9,500; multi-location restaurant site with online ordering at $18,000; full e-commerce platform migration at $32,000.

Specialty 07

Custom AI Agents.

$3,500–$25,000/build

Plus monthly platform/usage costs

When to use it

When your business has a repetitive knowledge-work problem an AI agent could absorb — first-touch lead qualification, customer FAQ handling, content generation in your voice, internal operational workflows. The right AI agent pays back fast; the wrong one is an expensive science project.

What's typically delivered

  • Use-case scoping and ROI modeling
  • Knowledge base ingestion
  • Voice and persona training
  • System prompt engineering
  • Tool and API integration
  • Workflow design and routing rules
  • Testing and evaluation harness
  • Deployment and access control
  • Documentation and team training
  • Performance monitoring setup

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Lead qualification chatbot at $4,800; brand-voice content agent at $8,500; multi-tool customer service agent with CRM integration at $18,000.

Specialty 08

Platform Integrations.

$1,500–$8,000/project

Per integration; complexity-dependent

When to use it

When data is trapped in disconnected systems — your booking software doesn't talk to your CRM, your POS doesn't sync to your loyalty program, your email tool is a separate island. Integration work is unglamorous and disproportionately valuable; it's what makes everything else compound.

What's typically delivered

  • Booking system integrations (Calendly, Acuity, OpenTable)
  • POS and payment integrations (Square, Toast, Stripe)
  • Loyalty platform connections
  • CRM-to-marketing sync
  • Email and SMS platform setup
  • Zapier / Make / native API workflows
  • Custom webhook implementations
  • Data flow documentation
  • Error handling and monitoring

Sample engagements

Sample engagements: Square POS to email platform sync at $1,800; multi-location booking to CRM integration at $4,200; custom loyalty platform tie-in at $7,500.

How engagements work

Scoped, signed, shipped.

Specialty work runs through a consistent process so there's no ambiguity on price, scope, or timeline. Your fCMO is in the room for scoping decisions — making sure project work supports the broader marketing plan rather than competing with it.

1. Scope. Your fCMO and the relevant specialist lead a scoping call to define the engagement — outcome, deliverables, timeline, and price. You get a written SOW before any work starts.

2. Sign. SOW is approved and the engagement is locked. Pricing is fixed for the scope; changes go through a formal scope-change process so there are no surprise overages.

3. Ship. The specialist team executes against the SOW with weekly check-ins. Your fCMO oversees alignment with brand and program priorities. You get a defined delivery and a wrap-up debrief at the end.

Specialty work is meant to layer in when needed and step out when it's done. The retainer programs handle continuity; specialty handles the moments where focused, time-bound expertise produces more value than ongoing capacity.
